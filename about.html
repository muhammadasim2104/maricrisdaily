About the journal

Hello. I’m Maricris.

This is my daily — a place for notes I do not want to lose to a camera roll or a busy week.

I write about home, the kitchen, slow hours, and the little joys that would never make an announcement. A first cup. A table left uncleared on purpose. Flowers bought on a day when nothing in particular had happened.

I started Maricris Daily because ordinary life disappears if no one writes it down. A meal becomes “we ate.” A morning becomes “I was busy.” I wanted a page that remembers the temperature of the day.

You will not find a performance here. Some notes are short. Some are only a mood with a date on it. That is allowed.

What lives here

A house, told in chapters.

  1. 01

    Mornings

    The pale hour by the window, before the house is fully awake.

  2. 02

    Home

    Rooms, cloths, corners, and the objects that have stayed.

  3. 03

    Kitchen

    Rice, garlic, a wooden spoon, and the room that tells the truth.

  4. 04

    Letters

    Notes addressed to a weekday, as if it might write back.

  5. 05

    Slow days

    The practice of leaving a soft hour unimproved.

  6. 06

    Little joys

    Flowers, small colors, and beauty that did not wait for an occasion.

If a sentence here keeps you company, I am glad. Come back when you can. The journal will still be on the table.

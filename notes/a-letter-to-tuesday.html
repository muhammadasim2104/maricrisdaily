Letters

A letter to an ordinary Tuesday.

21 October 20263 minute read

Dear Tuesday,

You are not the day anyone plans a life around. You arrive in the middle, with leftovers and a calendar that already looks full, and you ask to be lived anyway.

I used to hurry you. I treated you like a hallway between better rooms. I am trying to stop and put my hand on the wall. This is a room too.

Today there was coffee that went a little cold, a message I answered kindly, a shirt I finally hung up, and a laugh that did not come from a screen. None of that will be remembered as a story. I am writing it down so it counts.

I do not need a rare day. I need to be present for a common one.

Please do not become special. Stay ordinary, and I will meet you there.

Yours, while the light is still on the floor,
Mari Cris

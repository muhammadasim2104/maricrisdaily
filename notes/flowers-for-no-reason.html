Little joys

Flowers for no reason.

28 October 20263 minute read

I bought flowers on a day when nothing was being celebrated. The woman wrapping them asked if they were for someone. I said they were for the table. It sounded almost like an apology. It was not.

They were the pale ones, not the loud ones. I am often wrong about the names and right about wanting them in the house.

At home I cut the stems too short, then too long, then short again. I used the glass jug because the proper vase was somewhere I did not feel like searching. The jug was better.

For three days the kitchen looked as if a kinder person lived there. I did not become that person. I just walked past the flowers more often, and once I stood still and looked, which is a kind of thanks.

A small beautiful thing does not need an occasion. It can be the occasion.

When they tired, I did not feel I had wasted them. They had done their work. They had made an ordinary counter into a place I noticed.

— Maricris

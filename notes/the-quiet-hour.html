Slow days

The quiet hour.

2 November 20263 minute read

There is an hour in the late afternoon when the rooms get softer and I cannot tell if I am tired or simply done with noise. I used to fill it. I am practicing the opposite.

I put the phone in the other room, which is a small and slightly dramatic act, and then I do not become a better person. I just notice the refrigerator, a car outside, and my own shoulders.

Sometimes I fold clothes. Sometimes I sit on the floor with my back against the bed and do nothing that would look like rest to anyone watching. It is rest anyway.

The quiet hour is not a project. The moment I try to make it meaningful, it leaves. So I keep the rules few: a lower light, a slower pace, and no improving speech to myself.

Stillness does not have to be earned by a finished list.

If the hour is interrupted, it is still a good hour. I am learning to take the piece I am given.

— Mari Cris

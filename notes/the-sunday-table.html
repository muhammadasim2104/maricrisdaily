The Sunday table.

8 November 20264 minute read

Sunday has a sound in this house. It is the sound of a plate being set down a little more carefully than on a weekday, as if the table has been given the afternoon off too.

I wipe the surface even when it is already clean. I put out the cloth with the faded rose, the one I will not throw away. Someone always asks why we do not use the newer one. Because this one remembers more meals. That is the only answer I have.

We eat later than we mean to. There is rice, something green, something that took its time. I sit down before I remember the water jug, and then I get up again, which is also part of the ritual.

Afterward the table does not get cleared at once. Cups stay. A spoon stays. The light moves from one corner to the other, and I let it.

A table is not finished when the food is gone. It is finished when the room feels like itself again.

I think that is what I want these notes to do. Leave the day out a little longer, so I can see it.

— Maricris

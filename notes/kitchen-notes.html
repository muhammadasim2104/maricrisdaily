Kitchen

Notes while the rice cooks.

14 October 20263 minute read

The rice is doing that quiet work it does, and I am standing here with a spoon I do not need yet. The kitchen is the most honest room. It does not care if I am in a poetic mood. It wants the flame a little lower.

I wrote the first line of this on the back of a receipt. The oil smells like garlic and patience. A window is open. Somewhere a neighbor is living a life I will never know, and that thought makes the room feel larger.

People ask what I like to cook, as if the answer is a dish. The truer answer is the part where everyone is about to come and eat, and the part just after, when the pot is still warm and the day has been fed.

I taste the sauce with the wooden spoon and add salt the way a person does when she has learned, slowly, to trust her own mouth.

Feed the people, then write down how the room felt. In that order.

There will be dishes. There always are. They are not the opposite of a good evening. They are the proof that one happened.

— Maricris

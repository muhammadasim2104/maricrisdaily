Mornings

Morning light, before the house is awake.

12 November 20264 minute read

The house is still a different color at this hour. Not dark, and not really day. The window holds a pale gold that has not decided to be warm yet, and the kettle sounds louder than it will at noon.

I like this part. No one needs a decision from me. The cup is the same cup. The chair has the same little wobble. I sit in it anyway.

There is a theory that mornings should be improved. A colder shower, a list, a brighter plan. I have tried that life. It looks tidy from the outside and leaves me feeling as if I have already spent the day before it starts.

So I keep this hour plain. Water, a slice of something, the plant by the sink that is trying again. I open the curtain the rest of the way and let the light land where it wants.

I do not need the morning to be impressive. I need it to be mine for a few minutes.

A day can be ordinary and still be worth writing down. That is the whole idea of this journal. Not the finest moment. The one I was actually in.

If you are reading this later, I hope your own quiet part of the day is still there, waiting near a window.

— Mari Cris
